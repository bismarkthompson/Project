Sharp fades, steady hands, no rushed cuts.

Adum Barber House is a five-chair barbershop on the edge of Kejetia, run by barbers who trained on this street and still cut on it. Walk-ins are welcome; bookings get you straight into the chair.

What we do

  • Signature Fade

    A clean, blended fade cut to the shape of your head, finished with a straight-razor lineup around the hairline.

  • Beard Sculpt & Hot Towel

    Beard shaped to your jawline, hot towel prep, and a conditioning balm to finish. Pairs well with any haircut.

  • Kids' Cut

    Patient, quick cuts for children under 12, with a lollipop on the house and a parent-approved trim every time.
